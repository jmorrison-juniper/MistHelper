### WAN Performance migration

- **Added**: WAN Performance has four synthetic and cached live journeys, configured history, bounded gateway insights, and operator refresh. Port history stores six aligned 10-minute rate samples, raw scalar quality samples, local utilization summaries, sample-based congestion minutes, and event-derived status minutes and transitions. Operators can refresh one cached VPN peer path and retain documented loss, latency, and jitter scalars with their schema units. MOS retains its scalar score within the documented zero-through-five bounds, without inferred quality categories. Historical completeness and backfill remain unsupported. Detail pages show rolling 3-hour, 12-hour, and 24-hour summaries. Cached identity filtering, lexical sorting, ten-row pagination, and protected-text CSV export preserve full route and history scope. A separate read-only SLE form validates cached site membership and enabled catalog metrics before bounded requests. Duration units and completeness remain unverified. Issue #4200.
- **Changed**: The portal opens current cached observations, and four synthetic pages show labeled samples. Operators can select a cached site and view retained hours for one identity through `6h`, `12h`, `24h`, or `7d`. The UTC views keep missing hours explicit without inferred completeness, aggregation, or backfill. Organization/site aggregates are unavailable, and the cache table and CSV stay independent of these views. Source visual and functional equivalence are unverified. Issue #4200.
- **Fixed**: Empty gateway series preserve port and peer observations. Incomplete event and stats pages remain partial. Each stats collector caps a search at five pages and matches full peer identities locally. Missing rate samples do not count as healthy or complete congestion intervals. The gateway collector reads each requested rate from `results` and pairs it with `rt`. Retained missing MOS values with unknown units remain readable beside new scores without rewriting records. Daily, rolling, weekly, and monthly summaries select the documented score unit and still reject incompatible measured units. Issue #4200. Unchanged gateway samples replay without duplicate rows, and changed source data at the same sample key fails without replacement. Issue #4793. Mixed ordinary and event-complete hours retain unknown event coverage without a `KeyError` or fabricated healthy values. Issue #4794. Gateway history remains visible after an ordinary refresh and rejects ambiguous or foreign gateway scope. Issue #4795.
