The firmware version selected during the upgrade wizard.
Serial: Upgrades one device at a time, waiting for each to complete. Better for production networks. Parallel: Upgrades all devices simultaneously. Faster but higher network impact.
If enabled, devices that fail to upgrade will automatically revert to the previous firmware version. Rollback requires compatible firmware versions.
