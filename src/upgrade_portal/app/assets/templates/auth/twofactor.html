Why the page holds a literal path: contracts/http-api.md fixes every public path of this portal. The blueprint endpoint names are not fixed in the template layer, so the form action follows the contract path. url_for stays for a static asset. Why the page names no operator: The half-finished sign-in waits on the server, keyed by the first-party browser cookie.
